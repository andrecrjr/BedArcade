FOR PC GAMING TINKERERS

For the rabbit holes around your games.

From emulator menus and launcher settings to mods and game decompilation or recompilation projects, PC gaming can mean plenty of time on the desktop. Gamepad Desktop lets you move, click, scroll, and type in Windows and Linux apps with a gamepad. It’s free and open source under MIT.

GET STARTED

Up and running in three steps.

  1. 01Download your build

    Pick the Windows or Linux x64 package.

  2. 02Connect your controller

    Open Gamepad Desktop and select your gamepad.

  3. 03Settle in

    Move, click, scroll, and type from bed or couch.

Quick heads-up: Gamepad Desktop controls the desktop with mouse and keyboard input. It doesn’t add gamepad support to a game; the game needs to support a controller itself.

Linux setup notes

Linux needs input device permissions. The on-screen keyboard isn’t available on GNOME Wayland.
